Why We Exist

A Life Remembered.
A Mission Moving Forward.

In 2017, founder John Archer lost his daughter Skylar to a fentanyl-heroin overdose. Her memory became the foundation for a mission built around action, compassion, recovery, and another chance.

Today, Skylar's Hope For Change works to meet people where they are and connect them with practical pathways toward dignity, stability, health, and hope.

What We Do

Direct Support. Practical Pathways.

Six areas of service focused on bringing help closer to the people and communities that need it.

Mobile Dental & Medical Outreach

Bringing care directly to underserved neighborhoods, sober houses, and homeless communities.

Addiction Recovery Support

Connecting people with recovery resources, community, counseling partnerships, and long-term support.

Transitional & Sober Housing

Supporting safer housing pathways for people rebuilding their lives and stability.

Veteran & PTSD Support

Connecting veterans facing homelessness, trauma, and addiction with specialized resources.

Homeless Outreach

Delivering supplies, care, resource connections, and human connection where services often do not reach.

Community Partnerships

Building alliances with local professionals, businesses, churches, and nonprofit partners.
