Our Mission

Meeting People
Where They Are

Skylar's Hope For Change exists to meet people where they are — in recovery centers, underserved neighborhoods, homeless encampments, safe zones, and communities where help is often out of reach.

Through mobile health outreach, recovery support, transitional housing, and community partnerships, the organization is building practical pathways back to dignity, stability, and hope.

The Story Behind the Mission

She Was
Someone's Everything.

In 2017, founder John Archer lost his daughter Skylar to a fentanyl-heroin overdose. That loss became the foundation for a larger mission: to fight back against addiction, homelessness, and hopelessness with action, compassion, and real community support.

Skylar's Hope For Change carries her name as a promise — that her passing will not be the end of the story, but the beginning of something that gives others a chance to write their own.

What We Do

Our Programs

Six core areas of service, each designed to meet real needs with real action — not bureaucracy, not waiting lists.

Mobile Dental & Medical Outreach

Donated mobile buses bring dental and medical care directly to homeless encampments, sober houses, and underserved neighborhoods across Phoenix.

Addiction Recovery Support

Connecting individuals in active recovery with resources, community, counseling partnerships, and pathways toward stable long-term sobriety.

Transitional & Sober Housing

Supporting halfway houses, three-quarter houses, safe houses, and sober living environments — opening doors to stable housing for people in recovery.

Veteran & PTSD Support

Dedicated outreach to veterans experiencing homelessness, PTSD, addiction, and mental health challenges — connecting them to housing and specialized care.

Homeless Outreach

Direct engagement with homeless encampments and safe zones, delivering supplies, care, and human connection in places where most services don't reach.

Community Partnerships

Building alliances with churches, local businesses, mental health providers, dental professionals, and nonprofit partners to multiply impact across Arizona.
