Where It Goes

What Your Donation Funds

Mobile Outreach Operations

Fuel, insurance, maintenance, and supplies for mobile dental and medical outreach vehicles serving Phoenix-area communities.

Medical & Dental Supplies

Dental materials, medical consumables, medications, hygiene kits, and health supplies distributed during outreach runs.

Housing Program Development

Startup and operational costs for safe housing, sober living, and transitional housing programs for people in recovery.

Volunteer Coordination

Training, coordination, and logistics support for the volunteers who make outreach runs and programs possible.

Nonprofit Infrastructure

501(c)(3) development, legal and administrative costs that help Skylar's Hope operate as a credible, accountable organization.

Veteran Support Services

Resources, outreach, and housing support specifically for veterans experiencing homelessness, PTSD, and addiction in Phoenix.
